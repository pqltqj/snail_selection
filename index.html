How it works

Select: click a snail to inspect its shell and parent. Claw: click repeatedly to crack a shell. Thicker shells take more clicks.

Run: snails move and added crabs feed. Predation pauses automatically at 25 survivors. Click Snails reproduce to create two offspring per survivor; the parents die.

Reset trial: a new random trial with your current settings. Save evidence first. Changes to starting variation apply on reset.

Shell colours: a visual key for thickness, not a claim about real snail colours. Thickness units are arbitrary. This model simplifies real biology.

Research: select individuals, transfer them into tanks and advance days. Banded crabs supply cues without eating snails.

Downloads contain only data and notes you choose to save.
